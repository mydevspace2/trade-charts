Water Heater Age by Serial Number

Pick the brand on the rating plate. Each page explains where the date hides in that maker’s serial number, gives a decoder and links the manufacturer source.

BrandHow the date is coded (example)
Bradford WhiteExample: HK16433183 = H (2011) + K (October) = October 2011.
RheemExample: 1209D01234 = December 2009.
RuudExample: 0514A12345 = May 2014.
RichmondExample: 0818M12345 = August 2018.
GE (Rheem-built)Example: 0307X12345 = March 2007.
A.O. SmithExample: 203707002601001 = week 37 of 2020 (early September). Older: 2C062938405 = March 2006.
StateExample: 1715A012345 = week 15 of 2017. Older: J89xxx = October 1989.
American Water HeaterExample: 1924115263020 = week 24 of 2019. InterNACHI example: 8906xxx = 6th week of 1989.
WhirlpoolExample: 1103A12345 = week 3 of 2011.

Brands not listed

We only publish decoders for formats documented by the manufacturer or a recognised inspector association. For other brands (for example Rinnai, Navien or York) use the manufacturer’s warranty lookup with the full serial number, or call their support line.
