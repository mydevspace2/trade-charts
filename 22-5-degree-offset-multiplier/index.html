22.5° Offset Multiplier

The multiplier for a 22.5-degree offset is 2.613 (commonly rounded to 2.6). Shrink is 0.199 in per inch of offset depth (3/16 in on most bender charts).

Using a 22.5° offset

Distance between bends = offset depth × 2.613. For a 4 in offset that is 10.45 in. Add the shrink (0.80 in for 4 in) to your measurement to the obstruction. Remember each offset adds twice its angle to the 360° maximum between pull points.

Frequently asked questions

What is the multiplier for a 22.5 degree offset?
2.613 exact (1 ÷ sin 22.5°); bending charts round it to 2.6.
What is the shrink for a 22.5 degree offset?
0.199 in per inch of offset depth (field value 3/16 in per inch).
