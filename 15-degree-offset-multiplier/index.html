15° Offset Multiplier

The multiplier for a 15-degree offset is 3.864 (commonly rounded to 3.9 (some use 3.86)). Shrink is 0.132 in per inch of offset depth (1/8 in on most bender charts).

Using a 15° offset

Distance between bends = offset depth × 3.864. For a 4 in offset that is 15.45 in. Add the shrink (0.53 in for 4 in) to your measurement to the obstruction. Remember each offset adds twice its angle to the 360° maximum between pull points.

Frequently asked questions

What is the multiplier for a 15 degree offset?
3.864 exact (1 ÷ sin 15°); bending charts round it to 3.9 (some use 3.86).
What is the shrink for a 15 degree offset?
0.132 in per inch of offset depth (field value 1/8 in per inch).
