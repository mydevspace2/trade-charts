1/2" PVC Schedule 40 Conduit Fill

A 1/2-inch PVC Schedule 40 holds up to 8 × 12 AWG or 5 × 10 AWG THHN/THWN-2 conductors at the NEC 40% fill limit. Its internal area is 0.285 in² (40% = 0.114 in²).

Mixed sizes example

Three 10 AWG THHN plus one 12 AWG THHN ground: 3 × 0.0211 + 0.0133 = 0.0766 in², which is 26.9% of 1/2" PVC Schedule 40 (within the 40% limit). For mixed sizes always add the areas instead of using the same-size table.

Notes

  • Counts are for conductors only. Grounding conductors count toward fill.
  • With more than three current-carrying conductors, apply ampacity adjustment (NEC 310.15(C)(1)).
  • A nipple of 24 in. or less may be filled to 60%.

How many wires fit: Schedule 40 vs Schedule 80

At the 40% limit, 1/2 in. Schedule 40 PVC offers 0.114 sq in. of wire space and Schedule 80 offers 0.087 sq in., because the thicker wall of Schedule 80 leaves a smaller opening. Dividing by the THHN conductor areas in Chapter 9 Table 5 gives the maximum number of same-size wires:

Counts apply to all conductors of the same size and include grounds. For mixed sizes, add the areas and compare with the usable area, or use the conduit fill calculator.

Using PVC in practice

PVC is not a ground path, so count the equipment grounding conductor in the fill. Schedule 80 is the one to use where the conduit may be damaged, and expansion fittings are needed on long straight runs. The conduit comparison guide covers the rules, and the 40% rule guide explains the limit itself.
