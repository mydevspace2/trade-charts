Bradford White Serial Number: The 20-Year Letter Code Explained

Guide · Updated November 13, 2026

Bradford White makes dating a water heater easier than most brands: the first two characters of the serial number are the year and the month, and the manufacturer publishes the chart. The catch is that the year letters repeat every 20 years, so the same letter can mean two different decades. Here is how to read it and how to pick the right one.

Where to find the serial number

Look for the rating plate on the front of the water heater, near the controls. It lists the model number and the serial number. Bradford White’s own My Water Heater page shows where each sits. Use the serial number, not the model number.

The year letter (first character)

The first character is a letter for the year of manufacture. Bradford White does not use the letters I, O, Q, R, U or V for years, which leaves 20 letters. Each appears twice on the company’s chart, 20 years apart:

Worked examples

Ages below are as of November 2026.

  • HK16433183. H = 2011 (or 2031, which has not happened yet). K = October. So October 2011, about 15 years old.
  • TD… T = 2019, D = April. April 2019, about 7 and a half years old.
  • XB… X = 2021, B = February. February 2021, nearly 6 years old.
  • CE… C = 2006 or 2026, E = May. This could be May 2006, a 20-year-old heater, or May 2026, a brand-new one. This is the tricky case.

The decade problem: 2006 or 2026?

Because the letters repeat, a heater with an A, B or C year letter today could have been built in 2004–2006 or in 2024–2026. Every letter will have this issue as the years pass. Ways to decide:

  1. Condition. A tank that is 20 years old shows it: rust at the fittings and bottom, sediment noise, scale on the anode port, and a faded label. A 2-year-old tank looks clean.
  2. Warranty lookup. Bradford White’s My Water Heater page has a warranty search that takes the serial number. A real new heater returns warranty information.
  3. House history. If the home was built in 2015, a 2006 heater would have been moved there, which is unlikely.
  4. Paperwork. Permits, receipts and the installer’s sticker usually show the install date.
  5. Ask the manufacturer. Give Bradford White the full serial number.

Bradford White’s published chart begins at 2004. Older units use the same letter sequence but the company chart does not list them, so for a pre-2004 heater confirm the date with the manufacturer.

Sanity checks that catch misreads

  • If the first character is I, O, Q, R, U or V, it is not a Bradford White year letter. You may have read a different line, or the heater is another brand.
  • If the second character is a letter from N to Z, or the letter I, it is not a valid month. Recheck the characters, since a zero and an O are easy to mix up.
  • A heater that decodes to a date in the future is a wrong reading or the other decade.

What the age means

Tank-type water heaters are generally planned around 10 to 15 years of service, the range used on our decoder pages. Water quality and maintenance shift that a lot. If your heater is 15 or more, watch for water at the base, rumbling from sediment and rusty hot water. See how to tell the age of a water heater for the other major brands.

Frequently asked questions

How do I read a Bradford White serial number?
The first character is a letter for the year and the second is a letter for the month. H K means October 2011, for example.
Why does the same year letter appear twice?
Bradford White’s letters repeat every 20 years, so C is 2006 or 2026 on the company chart.
Which letters does Bradford White skip?
Year letters skip I, O, Q, R, U and V. Month letters skip I and everything from N to Z.
How do I tell which decade my heater is from?
Use its condition, the warranty lookup on Bradford White’s My Water Heater page, house history and installation paperwork.
Where is the serial number on a Bradford White?
On the rating plate on the front of the water heater, near the controls, next to the model number.
