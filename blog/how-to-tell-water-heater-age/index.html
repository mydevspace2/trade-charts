How to Tell the Age of a Water Heater From the Serial Number

Guide · Updated November 13, 2026

Most water heaters do not print a plain manufacture date. It is coded into the serial number, and each manufacturer uses its own code.

Find the rating plate

On a tank water heater the rating plate is the sticker on the front of the jacket, usually above the gas valve or the upper element cover. Copy the serial number exactly; do not confuse it with the model number.

The main formats

Brand familyCodeExample
Bradford White1st letter = year (20-year cycle), 2nd = monthHK… = October 2011 (or 1991)
Rheem, Ruud, RichmondMMYY at the start1209D… = December 2009
A.O. Smith, StateYYWW at the start (older: month letter + year)2037… = week 37 of 2020
American, WhirlpoolYYWW at the start1924… = week 24 of 2019

Each brand page has a decoder: Bradford White, Rheem, A.O. Smith, State, American and more.

Bradford White’s 20-year loop

Bradford White year letters repeat every 20 years and skip I, O, Q, R, U and V, so “H” can be 1991 or 2011. Use the condition of the heater, the energy label style and the home’s history to pick the decade.

Why it matters

The U.S. Department of Energy estimates 10–15 years of life for storage water heaters. Knowing the age helps decide whether to repair or replace, check warranty (registered warranties usually run from the installation date), and plan before a leak forces an emergency replacement.

When decoding fails

Use the manufacturer’s online warranty lookup with the full serial number, or call their support line. Store brands are made by one of the big manufacturers; the decoder for that maker usually works.

Signs it may be near the end

  • Rust-colored hot water or rust at the base of the tank.
  • Moisture or drips around the tank.
  • Rumbling or popping from sediment build-up.
  • An age at or beyond the 10–15 year range DOE gives.

Once you know the date, write it on the jacket with a marker so the next person does not have to decode the serial again.

HVAC equipment too

Furnaces, air conditioners and heat pumps also hide the date in the serial number, with different formats: Carrier, Goodman, Lennox, Trane and Rheem/Ruud.

Frequently asked questions

Where is the serial number on a water heater?
On the rating plate sticker on the front of the tank jacket, usually above the gas valve or the upper element cover. Do not confuse it with the model number.
How do I read a Rheem water heater serial number?
Rheem, Ruud and Richmond serials start with the month and year of manufacture: 1209 means December 2009.
How do I read a Bradford White serial number?
The first letter is the year on a 20-year cycle and the second letter is the month. Because the letters repeat, H can mean 1991 or 2011.
How long does a tank water heater last?
The US Department of Energy estimates 10 to 15 years for storage water heaters.
What if the serial number does not decode?
Use the manufacturer’s warranty lookup with the full serial number or call their support line. Store brands are built by major manufacturers and usually follow that maker’s format.
