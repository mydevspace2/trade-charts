10° Offset Multiplier

The multiplier for a 10-degree offset is 5.759 (commonly rounded to 6 (some use 5.75)). Shrink is 0.087 in per inch of offset depth (1/16 in on most bender charts).

Using a 10° offset

Distance between bends = offset depth × 5.759. For a 4 in offset that is 23.04 in. Add the shrink (0.35 in for 4 in) to your measurement to the obstruction. A 10° offset is long and gentle: good for small kicks at boxes but it needs more room.

Frequently asked questions

What is the multiplier for a 10 degree offset?
5.759 exact (1 ÷ sin 10°); bending charts round it to 6 (some use 5.75).
What is the shrink for a 10 degree offset?
0.087 in per inch of offset depth (field value 1/16 in per inch).
