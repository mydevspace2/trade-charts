Richmond Water Heater Age by Serial Number

Richmond water heaters are built by Rheem: first two digits = month, next two = year.

Decode a serial number

Example: 0818M12345 = August 2018.

Richmond serial number date examples

How the start of the serial reads for units built in different years (the rest of the serial is a production number and does not affect the date):

Digits that do not fit the pattern (for example a “month” of 14 or a week of 60) mean the serial uses a different or older format; check for a printed date on the label or ask the manufacturer.

How to read it step by step

  1. Find the rating plate and copy the serial number exactly (not the model number).
  2. Type it in the decoder above or read the characters described on this page.
  3. Sanity-check the result: the unit cannot be newer than the house addition it serves or older than the refrigerant/standards printed on the plate.
  4. If the plate shows a printed manufacture date, that date overrides any decoding.

How long do water heaters last? The U.S. Department of Energy puts the life of a storage (tank) water heater at roughly 10–15 years and tankless units at 20 years or more; water quality, maintenance and installation shift that a lot. Age alone does not mean a heater must be replaced, but an older tank with rust at the fittings, rumbling or water on the floor should be evaluated.

Where to find the rating plate

On tank water heaters the rating plate is the large label on the front of the jacket, normally above the gas control or the upper heating element cover. On tankless units it is on the side or inside the front cover. The plate lists the model number, serial number, capacity, input rating and the fuel or voltage; the serial number is usually printed with a barcode.

What to do with the age

  • Check warranty: register new equipment, and keep the installation invoice; many warranties are longer when registered.
  • Plan replacement before failure on older equipment, especially before summer or winter peak season.
  • Look up the right parts and service literature for that production period.
  • For home inspections, report the decoded date and the method used, and note any uncertainty about the decade.

Source

InterNACHI water heater dating chart (Richmond): www.nachi.org. Formats change over time; when in doubt, the manufacturer’s warranty lookup with the full serial number gives the authoritative date.

Frequently asked questions

How do I tell the age of a Richmond water heater?
Richmond water heaters are built by Rheem: first two digits = month, next two = year.
Where is the serial number?
On the rating plate: for tank water heaters it is the label on the front of the jacket near the controls; for outdoor units it is on the data plate on the side or back panel; furnaces have it inside the blower door.
Is the manufacture date the install date?
No. Units can sit in a warehouse for months. For warranty, manufacturers use the installation date if the product was registered, so keep your invoice.
