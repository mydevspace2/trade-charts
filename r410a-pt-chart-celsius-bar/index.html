R-410A PT Chart in °C and bar

At 45 °C, R-410A has a saturation pressure of 26.32 bar gauge (27.34 bar absolute) on the bubble (liquid) side and 26.25 bar(g) on the dew side. At 5 °C it is 8.32 bar(g) dew. The full table below covers every degree from -51 to 65 °C in bar gauge and bar absolute.

R-410A pressure-temperature lookup

For this zeotropic blend use the dew value for superheat (evaporator / suction line) and the bubble value for subcooling (condenser / liquid line). Valid -60 to 158 °F.

Gauge or absolute bar?

Manifold gauges read gauge pressure (bar(g)): zero at atmospheric pressure. Most European service data, compressor software and textbooks quote absolute pressure (bar(a) or bar abs), which is gauge pressure plus about 1.013 bar at sea level. If a spec sheet lists R-410A at 27.3 bar for 45 °C, it is absolute; your gauge will show about 26.3 bar. 1 bar = 100 kPa = 0.1 MPa = 14.504 psi.

Bubble or dew?

R-410A is a near-azeotropic blend of R-32 and R-125, so its bubble and dew pressures differ by only about 0.01–0.02 bar (glide under 0.2 K). Strictly, use the dew column for superheat (suction side) and the bubble column for subcooling (liquid side); in practice either is close enough.

Worked example (metric)

Subcooling. The liquid line gauge reads 26.3 bar(g). The chart gives a saturation temperature of 45 °C. The liquid line thermometer reads 40 °C, so subcooling is 45 − 40 = 5 K.

Superheat. Suction pressure is 8.3 bar(g), which is 5 °C on the dew column. The suction line reads 11 °C, so superheat is 6 K. Always compare with the equipment manufacturer’s targets. The superheat and subcooling calculators accept °C and bar.

How this chart was made and checked

Pressures are computed with CoolProp 8, an open-source property library that implements the same Helmholtz equations of state used by NIST REFPROP, at 1 °F steps with the ASHRAE Standard 34 blend composition. We then checked the result against published charts: Arkema Forane PT chart (2025, REFPROP 10) (41 points, max 0.09 °F). Across all 41 compared points the largest difference is 0.09 °F in saturation temperature (our pass limit is 1.0 °F, about the resolution of a good digital gauge). Points within 2 psi of atmospheric pressure are not compared because printed charts switch to vacuum units there.

Frequently asked questions

What is the R-410A saturation pressure at 45 °C in bar?
26.32 bar gauge (27.34 bar absolute) on the bubble side, and 26.25 bar(g) on the dew side.
What is the R-410A pressure at 40 °C in bar?
23.24 bar gauge, or 24.26 bar absolute (bubble).
What is the R-410A pressure at 5 °C in bar?
8.32 bar gauge on the dew side (9.33 bar absolute).
Are these values gauge or absolute?
Both are shown. Gauge (bar(g)) is what a manifold reads; absolute (bar(a)) adds 1.013 bar of atmospheric pressure at sea level.
Where do these R-410A numbers come from?
The same CoolProp 8 data as our °F chart (REFPROP-equivalent equation of state), checked against published manufacturer charts within 1 °F, then converted to °C and bar.
