Example. How many 12 AWG THHN fit in 3/4" EMT? The 40% fill area of 3/4" EMT is 0.213 in² and one 12 AWG THHN is 0.0133 in², so 0.213 ÷ 0.0133 = 16.02. Chapter 9 Note 7 allows rounding up only when the decimal is 0.8 or more, so the answer is 16 conductors.

How conduit fill is calculated

Add up the cross-sectional area of every conductor from Chapter 9 Table 5 (including the equipment grounding conductor), then compare the total with the allowable area of the raceway from Table 4: 53% of the internal area for a single conductor, 31% for two, 40% for three or more. When every conductor is the same size, divide the 40% area by one conductor’s area; Note 7 lets you round up if the remainder is 0.8 or more. Nipples 24 inches or shorter may be filled to 60% (Note 4).

Other wire types (TW, THW, RHH, bare conductors, cables) have different areas, so use the calculator with the closest type or the actual manufacturer diameter. Fill is only one check: also verify ampacity adjustment for more than three current-carrying conductors and the bending space at pull points.

Frequently asked questions

How many 12 AWG wires fit in 3/4" EMT?
16 THHN/THWN-2 conductors at 40% fill.
What is the 40% conduit fill rule?
NEC Chapter 9 Table 1 limits fill to 53% for one conductor, 31% for two and 40% for three or more conductors of the cross-sectional area of the raceway.
Do ground wires count in conduit fill?
Yes. Equipment grounding and bonding conductors, insulated or bare, are included when calculating fill (Chapter 9, Note 3).
Does conduit fill account for ampacity derating?
No. With more than three current-carrying conductors you must also apply the adjustment factors of NEC 310.15(C)(1), which may require larger wire.
