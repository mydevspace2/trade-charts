Why techs use Trade Charts

  • Checked data. Each PT chart is compared point by point with manufacturer or NIST data and shows its maximum deviation.
  • Two-way lookups. Temperature to pressure and pressure to temperature, °F/°C and psig/kPa/bar.
  • Code references on the page. NEC and IFGC table numbers and sources are printed with the numbers.
  • Fast and private. Static pages, no ads, no trackers, no cookies, no sign-up. Dark mode and print views built in.
