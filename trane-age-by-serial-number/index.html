Trane & American Standard Age by Serial Number

Residential Trane and American Standard units made from 2010 have a 10-character serial starting with year (two digits) and week (two digits). 2002–2009 units have a 9-character serial starting with a one-digit year and two-digit week. Older units use a letter code for the year.

Decode a serial number

Example: 1623xxxxxx = week 23 of 2016. 752xxxxxx (9 characters) = week 52 of 2007.

How to read it step by step

  1. Find the rating plate and copy the serial number exactly (not the model number).
  2. Type it in the decoder above or read the characters described on this page.
  3. Sanity-check the result: the unit cannot be newer than the house addition it serves or older than the refrigerant/standards printed on the plate.
  4. If the plate shows a printed manufacture date, that date overrides any decoding.

Why the age matters. Age helps decide repair versus replace, estimate remaining warranty (most manufacturer warranties run from installation date with registration, or from manufacture date if not registered), and tells you which refrigerant the unit likely uses: most residential A/C built before 2010 used R-22, units from 2010 to 2024 mostly R-410A, and new systems from 2025 increasingly use A2L refrigerants such as R-454B or R-32. Check the rating plate for the actual refrigerant.

Where to find the data plate

On outdoor condensers and heat pumps the data plate is on a side or rear panel, often near the service valves or the electrical access panel. On furnaces and air handlers it is inside the blower or burner compartment door. Write down the model number too: it tells you capacity (tons or Btu/h), efficiency series and the refrigerant.

What to do with the age

  • Check warranty: register new equipment, and keep the installation invoice; many warranties are longer when registered.
  • Plan replacement before failure on older equipment, especially before summer or winter peak season.
  • Look up the right parts and service literature for that production period.
  • For home inspections, report the decoded date and the method used, and note any uncertainty about the decade.

Source

Trane Commercial Help Center (serial date codes); inspector references: support.trane.com. Formats change over time; when in doubt, the manufacturer’s warranty lookup with the full serial number gives the authoritative date.

Frequently asked questions

How do I tell the age of a Trane & American Standard unit?
Residential Trane and American Standard units made from 2010 have a 10-character serial starting with year (two digits) and week (two digits). 2002–2009 units have a 9-character serial starting with a one-digit year and two-digit week. Older units use a letter code for the year.
Where is the serial number?
On the rating plate: for tank water heaters it is the label on the front of the jacket near the controls; for outdoor units it is on the data plate on the side or back panel; furnaces have it inside the blower door.
Is the manufacture date the install date?
No. Units can sit in a warehouse for months. For warranty, manufacturers use the installation date if the product was registered, so keep your invoice.
