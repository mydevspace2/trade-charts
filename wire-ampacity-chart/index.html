Using the chart correctly

Start from the column that matches the lowest temperature rating of any termination. Apply ambient correction (310.15(B)) and adjustment for more than three current-carrying conductors (310.15(C)(1)) to the insulation-rated column, then make sure the result is not above the terminal column. Remember 240.4(D) for small conductors and the 125% rule for continuous loads.

Frequently asked questions

What is NEC Table 310.16?
The base allowable ampacity table for insulated conductors rated up to 2000 V with not more than three current-carrying conductors in raceway, cable or earth at 30 °C ambient.
Why is 12 AWG on a 20 A breaker if the table says 25 A?
NEC 240.4(D) limits overcurrent protection for 14, 12 and 10 AWG copper to 15, 20 and 30 A in most cases.
Which temperature column should I use?
The lowest rating of conductor insulation, terminals and equipment. Circuits of 100 A or less generally use 60 °C unless equipment is marked 75 °C.
