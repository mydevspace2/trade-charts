About Trade Charts

Trade Charts is a free reference site for HVAC/R technicians, electricians, plumbers, gas fitters and welders in the United States. It is built to be fast on a phone, readable in sunlight or in the dark, and honest about where every number comes from.

Data sources

  • Refrigerant properties: CoolProp 8 (open-source implementation of the reference equations of state used by NIST REFPROP), ASHRAE Standard 34 blend compositions; R-438A from the DuPont/Chemours ISCEON MO99 chart.
  • Verification charts: Arkema Forane PT chart (2025, REFPROP 10), Honeywell Solstice/Genetron PT charts and pressure-based tool, Hudson Technologies PT charts, Chemours, NIST Chemistry WebBook, ASHRAE/UNEP refrigerant factsheet (2025).
  • GWP: IPCC AR4 values as listed by the UNEP Ozone Secretariat; ASHRAE/UNEP factsheet where noted.
  • Electrical: NEC (NFPA 70) Chapter 9 Tables 1, 4, 5 and 8; Table 310.16 (NFPA 70-2023 as printed in NFPA TIA 1691); 314.16 box fill tables.
  • Gas piping: 2021 International Fuel Gas Code Tables 402.4(1)–402.4(36).
  • Serial number formats: manufacturer documents (Bradford White, Rheem, A.O. Smith, Lennox, Trane, Carrier, Goodman) and the InterNACHI water heater dating chart.
  • Welding: Miller Electric published parameter guides.

How we check

Every PT chart is generated by a script and compared point by point against at least one published chart before it can go live; the build fails if any point differs by more than 1 °F. Code tables are parsed from the source documents and the published values are compared again in automated tests. We publish fewer pages rather than guess: refrigerants, brands and machines we cannot verify are left out and named as such.

What we are not
