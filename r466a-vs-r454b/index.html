R-466A vs R-454B

At 100 °F condensing, R-466A sits at 308.6 psig versus 303.3 psig for R-454B (+2%). At a 40 °F evaporator the dew pressures are 110.8 and 107.5 psig. R-466A is safety group A1, R-454B is A2L.

What changes in the field

Gauges and hoses. The pressures are close enough that the same high-pressure gauges and hoses work, but you must switch to the matching PT scale or refrigerant setting on your digital manifold.

Glide. At least one of these is a zeotropic blend with measurable glide: read superheat from the dew column and subcooling from the bubble column, and charge blends as liquid.

Flammability. ASHRAE safety group A2L: lower toxicity and mildly flammable (low burning velocity). Use A2L-rated recovery machines and leak detectors, remove ignition sources, ventilate the work area, and follow the equipment manufacturer’s A2L service instructions and UL 60335-2-40 / ASHRAE 15 requirements for the installation.

Is R-466A a drop-in for R-454B? Treat no refrigerant as a pure drop-in. Some blends are approved retrofits, but oil, seals, metering devices, pressure controls and charge amount may change. Follow the refrigerant producer’s retrofit guidelines and the equipment manufacturer.

Service checklist when you work on both

  • Confirm the refrigerant on the rating plate before connecting gauges; never rely on cylinder color or memory.
  • Use separate recovery cylinders for each refrigerant and label them; mixed refrigerant cannot be reclaimed normally.
  • Check that your recovery machine, vacuum pump and leak detector are rated for A2L R-466A and R-454B.
  • Charge blends as liquid and by weight to the nameplate charge, then confirm with superheat or subcooling.
  • Read pressures against the right PT chart; at 100 °F condensing the two differ by 5 psi.

Frequently asked questions

Does R-466A run at higher pressure than R-454B?
At a 100 °F condensing temperature R-466A is about 5 psi higher (+2%) than R-454B; at a 40 °F evaporator the dew pressures differ by +3.3 psi.
Which is safer, R-466A or R-454B?
R-466A is ASHRAE group A1 and R-454B is group A2L. A1 = no flame propagation, A2L = mildly flammable, A3 = highly flammable, B = higher toxicity.
Can I mix R-466A and R-454B?
No. Never mix refrigerants. Mixed refrigerant cannot be charged correctly or reclaimed normally and must be recovered as contaminated refrigerant.
